# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The condensed version:

## What we expect

- **Be kind.** Assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- **Be specific.** "This API design is confusing because X" is useful. "This is bad" is not.
- **Be patient.** Solo and small-team maintainers may take days to respond; that is the operating reality of a small project.
- **Be honest.** If you do not know, say so. If you are wrong, say so. The project's discipline is verify-before-claim — it applies to people too.

## What we do not accept

Harassment of any contributor. Personal attacks. Discriminatory or demeaning language. Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions. Disclosure of others' private information without permission.

If any of the above happens, the maintainer will address it privately first, then publicly if needed, and will remove access or block contributors who do not stop.

## How to report

Email the maintainer at **dep0we@gmail.com** with `[CoC]` in the subject. Include the relevant link (issue, PR, comment), what happened, and what outcome you are looking for. Reports are kept confidential by default; if you want a public outcome (e.g., the offending content removed and acknowledged), say so.

The maintainer will acknowledge within 7 days and resolve within 30. If the maintainer is themselves the subject of a report, escalate to the email above (the report still goes there) and expect a longer timeline while a neutral third party is consulted.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repo, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication channel the project hosts now or later. It also applies when contributors are representing the project in public.

## Enforcement summary

| Behavior | Likely response |
|---|---|
| One-off unkind comment | Private note from maintainer; expectation it does not recur |
| Pattern of unkind behavior | Public note + temporary cool-down (a week or two off the repo) |
| Targeted harassment | Removal of access; block |
| Doxing, threats, or anything that crosses a legal line | Removal of access; block; report to GitHub Trust and Safety and law enforcement where applicable |

Maintainer discretion applies. The goal is a project that is pleasant to contribute to, not a legalistic process.
