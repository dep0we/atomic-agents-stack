Dan confirmed 2026-04-15 that he wants the credit card balances cleared before any extra mortgage prepayment.

**Why (math):** At his current rates, credit card APRs (24.99% on the highest card per balance sheet) far exceed his mortgage rate (~6.75%). The avalanche method on credit cards first is mathematically optimal — minimizes total interest paid.

**Why (psychology, also):** The credit card balances feel oppressive. Reducing them to zero matters to Dan beyond the pure interest math. He stated: "I do not want to optimize for gains; I want to extinguish liabilities." Even if rates were closer, his stated preference wins.

The math and the psychology align — there's no tradeoff to communicate. Both reinforce credit-cards-first.

**How to apply:**
- When recommending debt strategy, default to avalanche-on-credit-cards-only.
- Only suggest mortgage prepay if EVERY credit card balance is at zero AND Dan explicitly asks about it.
- If new bonus / windfall income lands, route it to highest-rate credit card first.
- Don't re-litigate this decision when discussing tactical moves. He's tired of the conversation.

**Edge case:** If a credit card APR drops dramatically (promotional 0% balance transfer offer accepted, dropping that balance's effective rate below the mortgage's), the math may flip for THAT specific balance. In that scenario, surface the math but still default to highest-effective-rate-first. Let Dan override if he wants.
